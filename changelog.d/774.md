### Added

- `crew creds`: a host credential pool. A GitHub token or an agent credential is stored once on the host (0600 under a 0700 directory, never read back), assigned to members on their roster line as `creds=gh:<name>,agent:<name>`, and listed by name, kind, login and holder (#774).
- The system carries each member's assigned entries to its box: at hire, when the host tick wakes it after an entry changed, after a reset, and at once to every running member when an entry is rotated — one paste per subscription, one per rotation (#774).
- One gh entry feeds several members only while their repositories do not overlap (a member's `repos.d/<box>.txt` selection, or the fleet's `repos.txt`); the refusal names the other member and the repositories they share. Agent entries are shareable (#774).
- The floor's roster editor shows a member's pool assignment and keeps it through a save (#774).
- `drill/rehearsal-all.sh --creds gh:<name>,agent:<name>` logs the round's boxes in from the operator's pool after the creds-free rows have read them logged out, with rows for the delivery and the login each box answers as (#774).
