### Added

- The credential pool takes Telegram bots: `crew creds add tg <name>` stores a bot token (checked against Telegram's `getMe`), and `crew creds chat <id>` sets the fleet's one group chat. Each member is assigned a bot, and members may share one (#788).
- A box gets its bot and the group chat in the files `notify.sh` reads. A changed chat reaches every running holder at once. The floor's pool section manages both, and the drill's `--creds` takes a `tg:` entry (#788).
