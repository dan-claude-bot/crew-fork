### Added

- The handoff note: at every dispatch for a claim the engine fetches the pull request body's `## Worklog` and `## Round log`, keeps a copy beside the store (`.handoff-<repo>-<num>`, shipped by the probe) and injects it ahead of the repository in the resume and ci-red prompts; a session that ran to completion refreshes the copy (#760).
- Resume by default: a session that ended ok is continued by the next dispatch of the same kind and key — the vendor session resumed, bounded by `SESSION_CONTINUE_MAX_TRIES`, broken by any failure, skipped when the profile says the transcript is gone (#760).
- The wait idiom, engine-owned: `wait-for.sh checks|pid` polls a fact and never a command line, and `park-until.sh checks` parks a wait with the engine and ends the session; the builder's tick and the host's tick both read parked waits and dispatch the session again, resumed, with `why=wait#<repo>#<pr>`, the moment the checks conclude (#760).
