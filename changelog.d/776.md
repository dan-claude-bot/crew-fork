### Added

- `drill/rehearsal-all.sh --parallel` runs the three roles at once, each into its own transcript printed in role order when all end; the attention census grades suppression only for demands still standing after the tick, so a shared identity cannot red another role (#776).
- `--legs a,b,…` runs only the named legs, and `--only-failed <log>` re-runs a summary's FAIL and INCOMPLETE legs and lists every other leg as carried with that log's state (#776).
- `--fast`: the plumbing tier — roles in parallel, a zero-action agent, no app pass, and every section that reads a real agent's output skipped by name; its summary opens `## TIER: fast` and is never a promotion reading (#776).
- Every round ends with a `## wall time:` line per section and in total, and a wait of a minute or more prints its time on its row (#776).

### Fixed

- A drill wait whose upstream row already failed is refused at once as `blocked by: <row>` instead of polling out its budget (#776).
