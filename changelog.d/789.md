### Added

- The drill's `app-armed` leg runs on the round's own box by default. It arms the box's cron, moves its clock 120 s ahead as root, ticks it, compares, and then restores both; a box left armed or skewed fails the leg. `--no-app-armed-self` opts out (#789).
- The drill says at the start of a round whether the browser leg can run, naming a missing `playwright-core` (with the `npm i --no-save` line) or a missing Chrome (#789).
