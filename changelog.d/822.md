### Changed

- **Breaking:** crew is one service per machine. `install.sh` installs `/opt/crew` as root, and refuses a non-root caller that names no scratch prefix. There is no per-user install, and `~/.config/crew` and `$PWD` are no longer read (#822).
- Members of the `crew` group run `crew <verb>` as usual, and it re-runs itself as the `crew` account through a sudoers rule. Fleets live in `/var/lib/crew/fleets/<id>/`. With one fleet, `--fleet` may be left off; with several, a verb that acts on one fleet must name it (#822).

### Added

- `crew service setup` (as root) makes the `crew` account and group, `box grant --service crew --share crew` with default caps, `/var/lib/crew`, `/etc/crew`, the sudoers rule, systemd units and a first fleet (#822).
- The floor runs as `crew-floor.service`, and the schedule as the `crew-tick`, `crew-restart` and `crew-reset` timers, which catch up a run missed while the host was down (#822).
- `crew service check` reads the service back and exits 1 on drift, and `crew service teardown [--purge]` removes it. The drill gains a `service` leg that stands one up under scratch names and purges it (#822).
