### Added

- `crew up <box>` converges one roster member (create, start, hire) and touches no other (#815).
- The floor runs the CLI's own verbs as jobs: Up and Down for the fleet, plus Create (`crew up <box>`), Hire and Upgrade on each roster row. Up and Create show their dry-run plan first and run only once it is confirmed (#815).
- One job runs at a time on the whole host. A job bar shows the running one in every tab, with a spinner, the verb, the fleet, the elapsed time and the last line printed. Its output streams to a panel, and every job is journalled (#815).
- The drill gains two legs: `workspaces` (two fleets on one floor stay apart) and `floor-provision` (a fleet built and its member created, hired and logged in from the floor) (#815).
