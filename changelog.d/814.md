### Added

- Several fleets on one host. `crew init --fleet <id>` makes an empty fleet in the index (`~/.config/crew/fleets/<id>/`), and `crew --fleet <id> <command>` (or `CREW_FLEET`) runs a command against it (#814).
- The floor serves every fleet on the host: its own at `/`, each other one at `/f/<id>/`, with a switcher in the page and a New fleet entry. Two tabs can sit on two fleets (#814).
- A box belongs to exactly one fleet: `crew up`, `crew hire` and the floor's roster save refuse a box another fleet lists. `HOST_MAX_RUNNING` caps the boxes all fleets run together (#814).
- The host schedule (`crew tick`, `crew restart --all`, `crew reset --all`, with no fleet selected) walks every fleet in turn. `HOST_SCHEDULE=off` in a fleet's `fleet.conf` keeps that fleet off the walk (#814).
