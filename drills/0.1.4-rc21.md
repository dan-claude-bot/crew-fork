  `sudo crew service setup` makes the `crew` account and group, the
  `box grant --service crew --share crew` with caps, a sudoers rule for the

  `~/.config/crew-drill/credentials` (`drill/creds.sh`), used unless
  `CREW_CREDENTIALS_DIR` is set. Missing `--creds` entries, and the Telegram
  chat, are asked for on the terminal. Without a terminal, the round refuses
  and prints `drill/creds.sh …`. The floor's workspaces test also waits for a
  child floor's first snapshot (a CI race).

**The restart rule (#753, 2026-11-24).** A defect found during the week is
fixed on `main` as the next candidate, which replaces the running one on both
fleets. The seven days continue for a fix that leaves alone what runs inside
the boxes (docs, tests, the drill, host-side CLI or floor, the installer). They
restart for an engine change: `shared/bin/`, `shared/lib/` outside
`shared/lib/host/`, `shared/conf/` or `shared/prompts/`. The record names
which case each fix was, and a fix touching both restarts the clock.

If the round or the run finds a defect, the fix lands on `main`
(`0.1.4-rc22-dev`) and the next candidate is `0.1.4-rc22`. If it holds,
`0.1.4` is promoted stable from this candidate, by stamps only. A final may
differ from its last candidate by stamps only.
