## The round this file is waiting for

On the drill host, against the **published tag**, with a roster file that
carries at least one live armed member:

    drill/rehearsal-all.sh --ref 0.1.4-rc1 --app-roster <path>

- **`--ref 0.1.4-rc1` is required.** When this cut lands, the merge door re-arms
  `main` as `0.1.4-rc2-dev`. A bare invocation (`INSTALL_REF` defaults to
  `main`) would drill a tree that is not this candidate.
- **`--app-roster`** is what runs `app-armed`. The `0.1.3-rc3` round never
  passed it, so its A1 reading was never taken.
- **`release` (G3)** needs a triage identity that does not own its sandbox. The
  leg proves one cut per tick on a fresh track, held while its PR is open and
  refused once the PR is closed.
- **`notify`** needs the operator channel credential on the host. Without it,
  the leg skips as `operator channel unreachable: no-credentials`, as rc2's and
  rc3's rounds did. An unread reading is recorded as unread, never as a pass.
- **`fleet` (G2)** runs last, before teardown. On the round's own roster it
  covers `restart --all`, `down` and `reset --cut --all`: the per-box table, a
  busy box skipped and then waited for, a refusal recorded with its
  composition, the first tick after the `armed` restore, and the
  `duty-snapshot.*` count.
