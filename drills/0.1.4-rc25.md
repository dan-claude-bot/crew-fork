**The restart rule (#753, 2026-11-24).** A defect found during the week is
fixed on `main` as the next candidate, which replaces the running one on both
fleets. The seven days continue for a fix that leaves alone what runs inside
the boxes (docs, tests, the drill, host-side CLI or floor, the installer). They
restart for an engine change: `shared/bin/`, `shared/lib/` outside
`shared/lib/host/`, `shared/conf/` or `shared/prompts/`. The record names
which case each fix was, and a fix touching both restarts the clock.

If the round or the run finds a defect, the fix lands on `main`
(`0.1.4-rc26-dev`) and the next candidate is `0.1.4-rc26`. If it holds, `0.1.4`
is promoted stable from this candidate, by stamps only. A final may differ from
its last candidate by stamps only.
