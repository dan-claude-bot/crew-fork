## The waiver, stated so it is a reviewable commit and not a silence

At @danmt's direction on `2026-10-23`, `0.1.3` is promoted on the rc3
round's evidence alone. Gate B and Gate C are **waived**, not read: both
require a running fleet on `dev-server-1`, and the fleet was stopped on
`2026-09-13` for the reasons crew#753 records — the quota, the latency and
CI — and is not restarted for this final. The merge of the release PR is the
operator's ruling on this waiver; a reader who finds this file without that
merge should treat the waiver as proposed, not given.

What the waiver costs, named: B1's canary-first fleet upgrade with the
per-box integrity reading, B2's kimi boot check, B3/B4's `restart --all`
reclaim and `duty-snapshot.*` count, B5's `armed` cut and one restore,
B6/B8's crontab install, B7's kimi resume across a wall kill, and C1's flat
`/` across a week — none is read on this candidate. Every one of them is a
reading about a fleet that is running, and the next fleet that runs is
crew#753's (`0.1.4`, *the fleet comes back*), whose promotion gate is a
seven-day run on heavy-duty/la-familia-site. **The B and C readings this
record does not carry are owed on that candidate**, where there is a fleet to
take them from, and crew#753's init carries them as rows.

#656's hand checks (`crew restart --all`, `crew down`, `crew reset --cut
--all` on the round's fleet) are not taken either, for the same reason, and
#656 lands after this final as the stamps-only rule already placed it.

## The two harness findings, and why they land after this tag

#749 (`wait_for` conflates "not yet", "cannot grade" and "could not read")
and #750 (the ack and claim rows graded on a 300 s literal) are **harness**
findings: `drill/` is not a stamp path and `install.sh` excludes it from the
installed payload, so no engine byte is involved and fixing them cannot
re-open the rc3 evidence. They were held behind this final so that no
`drill/` byte lands between the rc3 tag and `0.1.3`, and their fix is opened
against `main` the moment the tag is published.
